AION Browser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AION Browser does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. You don't sign up for AION Browser.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads SDKs. AION Browser contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • No servers. AION Browser has no backend. Your browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, settings and website logins are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Websites you visit receive your requests directly, like in any browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Search goes to the search engine you choose in Settings (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Filter updates. To keep content blocking working, AION Browser may download public filter files from GitHub Pages (iliyarka87.github.io). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to GitHub, which operates that hosting. AION Browser sends no identifiers with these requests. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings.
  • Crash reports are only shared with the developer if you opted in to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (handled by Apple).

Children. AION Browser is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Contact: https://iliyarka87.github.io/aion-browser/ (support page) or the developer contact on the App Store page.
